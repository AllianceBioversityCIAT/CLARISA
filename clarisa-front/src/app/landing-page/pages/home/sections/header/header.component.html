What CLARISA is

CLARISA is the CGIAR Level Agricultural Results Interoperable System Architecture, a web service that helps collect and transform raw data of CGIAR research and activities into standardized and aggregated information.

In programming terms it is a REST-API that lets Management Information Systems and Centre specific systems communicate in the language needed for CGIAR system-level reporting.
